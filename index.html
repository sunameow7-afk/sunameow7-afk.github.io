How do I run DTF?
Open a ticket in our Discord and copy the install command, paste it into PowerShell as Administrator and press Enter. The encrypted payload downloads and DTF opens. To reinstall later, just run the command again.
Does DTF leave anything on my PC?
No. Closing DTF triggers a full self-clean (files, registry, prefetch, browser evidence, PowerShell history), then deletes itself, and a final verification scan confirms zero leftovers on screen.
How much does it cost?
₱500 ($8) for 1 day or ₱5,000 ($80) for lifetime — see the pricing on the DTF Cleaner page. Open a ticket, pick your plan, pay via GCash or PayPal, and your key lands in the ticket.
Is my license locked to one PC?
Yes. Every key is bound to your hardware ID (HWID) on first activation. One key, one PC. Moving PCs? Open a ticket and we can reset it.
Will antivirus flag it?
DTF is a cleaner that deletes files and edits the registry, and it is not code-signed yet, so some AVs flag tools like that by default. DTF never disables your antivirus, never adds exclusions for itself and never touches Defender's logs or history — if your AV blocks the download, allow that one file yourself in Windows Security > Protection history, or ask us in Discord before trusting it.
