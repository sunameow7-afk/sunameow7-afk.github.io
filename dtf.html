FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I run DTF?
Click Get DTF on this page — the dtf.bat launcher downloads once, and from then on you just double-click it. DTF opens, no commands needed. If you prefer a command, your ticket in our Discord includes one you can paste.
Does DTF leave anything on my PC?
No. Closing DTF triggers a full self-clean — files, registry, prefetch, browser evidence, PowerShell history — then deletes itself, and a final verification scan confirms zero leftovers on screen.
How much does it cost?
Keys are time-based (day plans available) and sold in our Discord. Open a ticket, pick your plan, pay via GCash or PayPal, and your key lands in the ticket.
Is my license locked to one PC?
Yes — every key is bound to your hardware ID (HWID) on first activation. One key, one PC. Moving PCs? Open a ticket and we can reset it.
Will antivirus flag it?
DTF is a cleaner that deletes files and edits the registry — some AVs flag tools like that by default. The launcher adds a Defender exclusion for DTF's folder automatically, and our Discord support walks you through it if your AV asks.
What's coming next from DTF?
